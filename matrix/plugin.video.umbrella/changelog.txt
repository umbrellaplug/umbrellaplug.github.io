[B]Update - v6.8.02 - Nov 16th, 2026[/B]
--[Fix] Resume points work again when an episode or movie is started from a skin widget, an info screen Play button or a skin helper. Umbrella was treating these launches as "Start from beginning". Applies to Trakt, Simkl, MDBList and the other resume sources.
--[Fix] MDBList now keeps only the most recent resume point for each episode or movie.
--[Fix] Episode lists should no longer duplicate after finishing an episode or show, which happened most often with Simkl. Home-screen widget refreshes now wait until Kodi has finished reloading the list after playback.

[B]Update - v6.8.01 - Nov 14th, 2026[/B]
--[Feature] Added a read-only mode for Trakt (Settings > Online Services > Trakt). Umbrella still reads your Trakt watched status, lists and resume points but stops reporting playback and marking items watched automatically, so another add-on such as the Trakt add-on can handle that. Marking items watched manually still works.
--[Fix] Progress Episodes and Progress Shows load much faster, especially on low-power devices such as Fire TV. They now reuse the last list until your watch history or dropped shows change, instead of rebuilding it on every load.
--[Fix] Simkl no longer reloads every home-screen widget every 30 minutes when nothing has changed.
--[Enhancement] Progress Shows now lists a show at the top for a week when its new season premieres, matching Progress Episodes.
--[Fix] Trakt watched indicators and the Trakt Progress and Watched show lists now include every show. Umbrella stopped after the first 100 shows because Trakt returns at most 100 per page.
--[Fix] MDBList Finish Watching no longer fills up with episodes and movies you finished. Umbrella now closes the MDBList playback session when an item is completed, and resuming past 80% no longer marks it watched early.
--[Feature] Added MDBList Tools > Clean up finished items in MDBList Finish Watching (also in Settings > Shortcuts). It removes items you already finished that older versions left in Finish Watching, and keeps unfinished items and rewatches in progress.
--[Fix] Account sync no longer stalls for minutes when Trakt rate-limits requests, so Custom, Floppy, Scrob and PunchPlay sync on time and Kodi closes promptly. Trakt rate-limit waits are now logged, capped at 60 seconds and retried at most twice.
--[Fix] Simkl Progress Episodes now loads shows in batches using the batch size setting instead of all at once, which avoids rate limits.
--[Fix] Progress Episodes no longer hides shows with episodes left to watch on devices where date parsing fails, such as Fire TV.
--[Fix] Fixed the same date-parsing failure in the PunchPlay calendar, episode air time labels and time conversion.
--[Fix] Watched checkmarks now update after playback even if a brief loading spinner is showing when the list refreshes.
--[Fix] Season counts for shows without an IMDb ID stay correct when a new episode airs.
--[Fix] Floppy, Scrob and PunchPlay Progress Episodes now use each show's broadcast time and timezone, so new episodes no longer appear up to a day early outside the US.
--[Fix] Speed up cached season and episode navigation by deferring HTTP setup and reusing watched progress within each episode list.
--[Fix] Limit repeated service requests during outages and retain saved show progress when the service cannot be reached.
--[Fix] Prevent delayed playback refreshes from interrupting Back navigation and skipping the Progress Episodes list.
--[Fix] Prevent incomplete Trakt activity responses from interrupting Progress Episodes list loading; cached results remain available.
--[Fix] Log uploads now clearly confirm completion and explain how to use the link. Fixed clipboard copying on cancellation and added an upload timeout.
--[Fix] Restore the saved language invoker preference after updates without forcing a profile reload. The restored preference takes effect on the next normal Kodi restart.
--[Fix] Prevent artwork and version lookups from failing during add-on reloads and interrupting startup or account sync.
--[Fix] Floppy sync cancellation during shutdown or profile reload no longer logs an error.
